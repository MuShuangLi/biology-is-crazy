Research & Development

"biology-is-crazy" is a highly advanced biological problem-solving system.

Developed in Python, the project attempts to apply the power of computer science to systematically analyze the vast and historically complex field of biology and produce a clear, reliable, and reproducible conclusion.

After extensive research, development, contemplation, and approximately one line of code, the project successfully achieved its objective.

The core algorithm is shown below:

print("不要學生物")
